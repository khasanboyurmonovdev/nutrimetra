# Quality, Nutrition Safety and Privacy Checklist

## Nutrition estimation
- [ ] Estimates visibly labeled as estimates.
- [ ] User can inspect and correct food identity, portion and preparation.
- [ ] AI never invents certainty about hidden ingredients or exact weight.
- [ ] Nutrient arithmetic is deterministic after food/portion resolution.
- [ ] Each value has provenance: database, label, user input or model estimate.
- [ ] Benchmark includes diverse cuisines and mixed dishes.
- [ ] Evaluation reports methodology, sample size and error distribution.

## Health and wellbeing
- [ ] Adult-first MVP and age-appropriate store rating.
- [ ] No medical diagnosis or treatment claims.
- [ ] Avoid aggressive calorie targets and extreme weight-loss language.
- [ ] Avoid shame, punitive streaks and moral labels for food.
- [ ] Sensitive-risk pathways are reviewed by qualified specialists before launch.

## Privacy and security
- [ ] Data minimization and clear consent.
- [ ] Private storage and short-lived signed URLs for meal photos.
- [ ] No sensitive payloads in analytics/crash logs.
- [ ] RLS and authorization tests for every user-owned resource.
- [ ] Account export and deletion.
- [ ] Third-party processor and retention disclosures.
- [ ] Store privacy labels accurately reflect actual SDK behavior.

## Release readiness
- [ ] Terms and privacy policy reviewed by qualified counsel.
- [ ] Food database licenses and attribution obligations reviewed.
- [ ] App Store and Google Play health/fitness policies reviewed.
- [ ] Subscription terms, trial disclosures and cancellation flow tested.
- [ ] Accessibility pass on real devices.
