# Brand System — NutriMetra

## Name
**NutriMetra** (working name; pending legal and platform clearance)

The name combines nutrition + measurement/intelligence. It signals understanding and clarity rather than dieting or restriction.

## Tagline
**Eat with clarity.**

## Brand attributes
- Calm, intelligent, encouraging
- Precise but never clinical or cold
- Modern, global and culturally inclusive
- Transparent rather than overconfident
- Supportive rather than judgmental

## Visual direction
- Deep teal: `#063F3B` — trust, focus, primary text
- Fresh green: `#35C98A` — progress and positive action
- Lime: `#B7EF45` — accent / active indicator
- Warm ivory: `#F8F6EF` — primary light background
- Coral: `#FF8068` — sparing highlight, never a failure state
- Slate: `#52615E` — secondary text
- White: `#FFFFFF` — surfaces

## Logo concept
A continuous circular mark with a leaf-like sweep and small orbiting point: a visual metaphor for food, measurement, and an ongoing personal feedback loop. Use a simplified symbol-only variant for app icon; avoid tiny tagline text in the icon.

## Typography
Use a clean, highly legible sans serif. Suggested: Inter or a platform-native system font. Use tabular numerals for calorie and macro values.

## Voice
Say: "You're close to your protein goal. A protein-rich snack could help."  
Do not say: "You failed your diet" or "bad food."
