# Product Brief

## Vision
Make nutrition understandable and sustainable for everyday people, regardless of cuisine, cooking style or fitness goal.

## Problem
Traditional trackers often require searching databases, guessing portions and manually entering ingredients. Photo-first trackers reduce friction but can create false confidence when a two-dimensional image is used to infer hidden ingredients, depth, weight, oil and cooking method.

## Target users (initial hypotheses)
1. **Busy health-conscious adults:** want awareness without spending several minutes logging each meal.
2. **Strength and fitness beginners:** need protein and calorie consistency without spreadsheet-level detail.
3. **People eating diverse home-cooked food:** need recognition beyond common Western packaged meals.
4. **Former tracker abandoners:** quit because logging felt tedious or judgmental.

Do not market to minors or people seeking treatment for eating disorders in the MVP.

## Positioning
**NutriMetra — Eat with clarity.**  
An intelligent nutrition companion that understands meals in context, shows its working, and helps users make the next decision.

## Differentiation hypothesis: Meal Context Engine
The moat is not "take a photo and get calories." That is becoming table stakes. The product should build a trustworthy personal model of:
- frequently eaten meals and user's usual portions;
- ingredients and cooking styles the user confirms;
- local and culturally diverse dishes;
- meal timing and user goals;
- corrections and confidence over time.

When uncertainty is high, the system asks one useful question, such as "Was this cooked with a tablespoon or closer to three tablespoons of oil?" It should not interrogate the user for every meal.

## Core loop
Capture → Identify → Estimate → Confirm/edit → Understand today's balance → One helpful suggestion → Return.

## MVP promise
"Log a meal in under 20 seconds, with estimates you can inspect and correct."

## MVP features
- Onboarding: age gate (18+ initially), units, goal, activity, dietary preferences and optional body metrics.
- Daily dashboard: energy estimate, protein/carbs/fat, meal timeline and remaining target.
- Meal capture: photo, text and manual entry.
- Structured AI recognition: candidate foods, preparation assumptions, portion estimate, confidence and uncertainty notes.
- User correction: change food, portion, ingredients and preparation.
- Food data lookup: verified database records and source attribution.
- Meal memory: save confirmed meals and portions for fast repeat logging.
- Weight trend: optional, user-controlled, trend-based display.
- Weekly reflection: patterns and one practical suggestion.
- Privacy controls: export/delete account; photo retention setting.

## Explicitly out of MVP
- Medical nutrition therapy or disease-specific recommendations.
- Automatic diagnosis or lab interpretation.
- Social feed, public leaderboards or competitive weight-loss challenges.
- Wearable integrations beyond a minimal post-MVP spike.
- Barcode catalog at global scale before food-data licensing and quality are assessed.
- Meal plans that claim clinical personalization.

## Success metrics
**Activation:** user completes onboarding and confirms first meal.  
**Core value:** median time to confirmed meal log; share of AI estimates edited; successful repeat-meal logs.  
**Retention:** D1/D7/D30 retention; meals logged per active user per week.  
**Trust:** estimate correction rate by food category; user-reported confidence; deletion/export completion.  
**Monetization:** trial-to-paid, paid retention, AI cost per active subscriber and gross margin.

Avoid optimizing raw scan count if users do not trust or retain the results.
